About Berlin Cricket Academy

Founded in 2020, Berlin Cricket Academy (BCA) is Berlin's first and only cricket club established specifically for kids and women, with strong participation from men. In just five years, we've grown into a vibrant community of 75+ members, receiving over 400 membership requests in 2025 alone.

Our members come from diverse backgrounds and nations, united by their passion for cricket. We train at the iconic Olympiastadion in the heart of Berlin and embark on annual international tours to Romania, Prague, and Poland, giving our players invaluable European cricket experience.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced player, BCA offers quality coaching, competitive opportunities, and a welcoming community where everyone belongs.

Our Mission

"Cricket For All" - We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to experience the joy of cricket, regardless of age, gender, background, or skill level.

Our mission is to develop cricket in Berlin by providing quality coaching, creating competitive opportunities, and building an inclusive community that celebrates diversity. We are committed to nurturing young talent, empowering women in cricket, and representing Berlin on the European stage.
