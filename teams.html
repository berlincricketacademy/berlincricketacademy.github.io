Our Teams

Berlin Cricket Academy welcomes players of all ages and abilities across our extensive range of teams. We provide a welcoming, fun and competitive environment to allow every player to improve and fulfil their potential.

🏆

Men's Regionalliga Team

Our primary men's team competing at the highest level in Regionalliga. Open to senior players of all abilities looking for competitive cricket.

📍 Elite Competitive Level

👥

Men's Verbandliga Team

Our secondary men's team competing in Verbandliga. Perfect for players developing their skills and gaining match experience.

📍 Development Level

👩

Women's Team

We actively encourage all girls and women from complete beginners to experienced players. No equipment necessary - we provide everything you need.

📍 Champions of East German Bundesliga

🧒

Youth Team (U19)

Welcome all junior players aged 8-19 of all abilities in a safe and friendly environment. We align with Cricket Germany talent pathway opportunities.

📍 Future Stars Development
