About Berlin Cricket Academy

Founded in 2020, Berlin Cricket Academy is Berlin's first and only cricket club established specifically for kids and women, with strong participation from men. We've grown into a vibrant community of 75+ members, receiving over 400 membership requests in 2025.

We train at the iconic Olympiastadion and embark on annual international tours to Romania, Prague, and Poland.

Train at the Olympiastadion

Experience world-class cricket training at Berlin's legendary Olympiastadion, where sporting history comes alive. Our outdoor sessions bring together Olympic grandeur with cricket excellence.

International Cricket Tours
